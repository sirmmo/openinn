The Slow Orbit

You run the bar and bunkhouse on a refuelling ring at the edge of the Tessel mining belt. At station night the airlock opens, and freighter pilots, belt miners, courier robots and the odd envoy come aboard: thirsty, hungry, tired, or running flat.

  1. On the dock shift, build and refit. Build out the ring next to what is there, cell by cell, then fit it out: a drinks counter and mess tables in the lounge, a food printer and a cold store in the galley, fizz tanks in the hold, sleep pods and berths in the cabins. Everything costs credits.
  2. At station night, the airlock is open. Guests head for whatever serves their most pressing need, pay and are served. The tanks brew fizz and the printer prints meals from your rations all night, as long as there are credits to pay for them.
  3. Keep the air clean. Everyone who breathes adds CO2, and it hardly leaks away on its own (see Show). Air scrubbers clear the rooms around them. Envoys hate stale air and noise.
  4. Charge the robots. Courier robots don't eat or drink, but they need a charging post, and they leave a bad review if they can't find one.
  5. Ratings. Happy guests raise your rating, which brings more ships, and pickier ones: belt miners at 10, envoys at 25. Guests who wait too long leave angry.

A season is seven nights. The game saves itself as you play.
