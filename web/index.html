    How to play

    1. By day, dig and furnish. Dig rooms next to the inn, cell by cell, then furnish them.
    2. By night, the doors are open. Guests walk to whatever serves their most pressing need, pay and are served.
    3. Reviews. Happy guests raise your reputation, which brings more guests.

    Controls: click a glowing square to dig or build; drag to move the view, right-drag (or two fingers) to turn it, wheel or pinch to zoom; space pauses the night. R (or Turn) turns furniture; click the inn's name to give it your own.

    Every rule of the inn is a GlassBox script; OpenInn's documentation explains how to write your own. Code: GPL-3.0.
